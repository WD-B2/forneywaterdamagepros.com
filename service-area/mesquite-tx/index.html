The water problems Mesquite homeowners call about

Mesquite is the largest city around us, a Dallas County city of about 150,000 people at the junction of I-635, I-30, I-20 and US 80. A city that size brings a mix of problems, from a split pipe in a single-family house to a storm-driven leak in a retail unit. The ones we hear most often involve floodwater and sewer backups after heavy rain, plumbing failures and roof leaks.

A big city also means a big sewer system. Mesquite's wastewater is treated at the Mesquite Regional Treatment Plant on Lawson Road, operated by the North Texas Municipal Water District. When a main backs up, the water can come up in several homes on one street. If that happens, tell the city as well as us, because the main is the city's to clear.

When water is spreading in Mesquite

Water that keeps spreading is an emergency, so the first minutes matter more than the first hour. Close the main valve, switch off the breakers for wet rooms if the panel is dry and call the line. For the street side of the system, the City of Mesquite lists a 24-hour water and sewer emergency number at 972-216-6278 and a call center for main breaks and emergency shutoffs at 972-288-7711.

We cover Mesquite from our Forney base about 12 miles away along US 80. The line is open 24/7. Response depends on crew availability, and we never promise an arrival time.

If water is still spreading in Mesquite: what to do while you wait

Work through these in order.

Find the shutoff

The main valve is usually near the front of the house, in the garage or beside the water heater.

Cut power to wet areas

Only if the panel is dry. Otherwise stay out and call the utility or 911.

Raise valuables

Electronics, paper and soft items go up first.

Avoid dirty water

If it came from a drain, a toilet or the yard, keep everyone away from it.

Record the damage

Photos and video with the date, before cleanup.

Storms, flooding and the weather in Mesquite

Mesquite sits at the lower end of Tornado Alley, according to Wikipedia, and severe storms bring wind and rain together. When wind opens a roof or breaks a window, rain follows, and the first sign is often a stain on a ceiling. The storm water damage page explains what happens next. When water comes in at ground level, the steps on the flood damage cleanup page apply.

The February 2021 freeze also reached Mesquite. The city issued a boil water notice that month. For homeowners the lesson is that supply interruptions and pipe breaks often come together, so check for hidden leaks after the water comes back.

Businesses, malls and corridors in Mesquite

Mesquite has a large retail and business base. Town East Mall opened in 1971, and the Mesquite Championship Rodeo, the Mesquite Arts Center and Eastfield College are all in the city. Large buildings like these have big floor areas and shared plumbing, so water loss in a business is a different job from a house, with tenants, landlords and insurers all involved.
