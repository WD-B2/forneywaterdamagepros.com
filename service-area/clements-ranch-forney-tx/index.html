Who do you call about water here?

Some listings for Clements Ranch homes show municipal utility district water and sewer service. That matters for a leak, because the district and not the city may own the line up to your meter. Check your water bill for the name of the provider before you call anyone about a main or street-side leak.

Inside the home, the leak is the homeowner's problem whatever the provider. Call us for the cleanup and the provider for anything on its side of the meter.

Slab floors and hidden leaks

Homes on a slab have no crawl space under the floor. When a supply line under the slab leaks, signs can include a warm spot on the floor, the sound of running water with every tap off, a sudden rise in the bill or a wet patch on the carpet.

A plumber finds and repairs the line. A restoration crew then dries the floor and the walls around the leak. The slab leak page explains how the two jobs fit together.

If you suspect water under the floor

Work through these in order.

  1. Check the meter

    With every tap off, watch the meter. If it still moves, water is escaping somewhere.

  2. Close the main

    Close the main valve to stop the loss until a plumber arrives.

  3. Keep people off wet floors

    Standing water near outlets is a shock risk. Keep clear, and call the utility if you smell burning or see sparks.

  4. Photograph and record

    Take photos of the wet area and note when you first saw it.

  5. Call the line

    Give the address and what you saw. We explain the drying side while you arrange the plumber.

Drying and the record for your insurer

Drying follows the readings. Air movers and dehumidifiers run until the walls and subfloor match dry targets, and the crew records the numbers each day. That log, plus photos and an itemized scope, can help describe the work for an insurer.

Coverage depends on the cause of the water and on your policy. Many policies treat a sudden break differently from a slow leak. Neither we nor this site can decide coverage or promise what an insurer will approve.
