Where the Forney line reaches

Our address is 724 E US Highway 80 in Forney, Texas, and US 80 is the spine of the area we cover. It runs about 20 miles west to Dallas and about 11 miles east to Terrell. Most of our work happens along it, in Forney itself and in the towns on either side.

Why the town matters to a water loss

Who owns the pipe, who answers the utility line and which insurer rules apply can change from one town to the next. In some places the meter marks the boundary of the homeowner's responsibility. In others a municipal district or supply corporation serves the street instead of the city.

That is why each town page lists local details and a short list of what to check. If your question is about a source of water, the burst pipe, water heater and storm water pages cover what to do first.

Calling from outside the listed towns

Water does not stop at a city limit. If you are in a neighboring community along US 80, call the line anyway. We will ask where you are and tell you plainly whether we can help and what to expect.

Coverage depends on crew availability on the night you call. If we cannot reach you, we will say so and suggest what to do in the meantime, such as shutting off the water and photographing the damage.

What to have ready when you call from a nearby town

Give the street or the nearest cross street, the source of the water if you know it and which rooms are wet. Mention whether anything electrical is wet and whether the water came from a drain or from outdoors. That short description lets us choose the right first instructions.

If you are in Terrell, Mesquite or Seagoville, also tell us whether your water comes from the city or from a district, because that decides who to call about the meter. Photographs taken before cleanup help your insurer, and we will remind you to take them.

Planning ahead for the next storm or freeze

The towns along US 80 share the same weather, with spring thunderstorms, hot summers and the occasional hard freeze. A small amount of preparation pays off in all of them. Learn where your main shutoff is, clear gutters before the rainy season and wrap exposed pipes before a freeze.
