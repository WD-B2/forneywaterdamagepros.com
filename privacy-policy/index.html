Privacy Policy

What we collect when you call us or visit this website, and how we use it.

Call (844) 950-6900

Overview

This policy explains what Forney Water Damage Pros collects when you visit this website or call our line. We keep it simple because the site is call-only and has no forms.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Information you give us by phone

When you call, we may collect your name, phone number, address, a description of the damage and details about your insurance if you share them. We use this to understand the problem, to line up a crew and to follow up about your job.

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the crews, insurers and service providers involved in your job, or when the law requires it.

Information collected by the website

The website has no forms, accounts or comment sections. Your browser may request fonts from Google Fonts and maps from Google Maps, and those services receive standard connection data such as your IP address under their own policies.

We do not use the site to build advertising profiles. If we add analytics later, we will update this page.

Your choices and contact

To ask what we hold about you or to request deletion of your information, email contact@forneywaterdamagepros.com or call (844) 950-6900. We will respond within a reasonable time.

We may update this policy. The current version is the one on this page. This policy was last updated on November 13, 2026.
