How to Shut Off Your Home's Water Main in Forney, TX

The fastest way to limit water damage is to stop the water. Learn where your shutoff is before you need it.

Call (844) 950-6900

Written by the Forney Water Damage Pros team. Published November 13, 2026.

Why this one skill matters

Every minute a pipe runs, the damage grows. A burst supply line can release a large volume of water in a short time, soaking floors, walls and ceilings. Stopping the flow is the single most useful thing anyone in the house can do.

Most people only look for the main shutoff when water is already spraying. Finding it on a calm day takes ten minutes and removes the panic. Everyone who lives in the house should know where it is and how it works.

Where to look for your main shutoff

In many Texas homes the main shutoff is near the water meter at the front of the lot, inside a ground-level box with a lid. There can also be a valve where the water line enters the house, often in a utility room, a garage wall, a closet or near the water heater. Some homes have both.

Many homes keep the valve in a box near the front corner of the yard, while older homes may have it in a crawl space or on an exterior wall. If you cannot find it, ask the previous owner or a plumber to locate it and tag it.

Restoration crews work to the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard, which starts with stopping the water source and then extracting and drying. The advice is the same for a house in Terrell.

Types of valves you may find

The type decides how you turn it.

ValveHow it looksHow to close it
Ball valveA lever handleTurn the lever a quarter turn so it sits across the pipe.
Gate valveA round wheel handleTurn clockwise several times until it stops.
Meter box valveSmall valve beside the meterUtility-owned in many cases, use only the customer side.

How to shut off the water

Stay calm and work through this order.

  1. Go to the main valve

    Head to the valve inside the house if you can reach it quickly, or to the meter box outside.

  2. Close it firmly

    Rotate a ball valve a quarter turn or a gate valve clockwise until it stops.

  3. Open a faucet

    Open the lowest faucet in the house to drain what is left in the pipes.

  4. Check the leak has stopped

    Look at the source. If water still runs, the valve may not be fully closed.

  5. Document and call

    Photograph the damage and call for water extraction and your insurer.

What belongs to the utility and what belongs to you

In most Texas cities the meter and the valve beside it belong to the utility, and the line past the meter is the homeowner's responsibility. Utilities generally ask customers not to operate their own valve at the meter. Some homes also have a separate shutoff on the customer side of the meter, which is yours to use.

Not every house near Forney gets water from the same provider, because some neighborhoods are served by a municipal utility district or a supply corporation. Check your water bill to see who your provider is, and call them if you cannot reach or close your own valve.

If the valve is stuck or leaking

Valves that go years without use can seize. Do not force a stuck handle with a wrench, because you can break the stem and make a bigger problem. Try gentle back and forth movement and a spray of penetrating oil on a gate valve stem.

If it will not turn, call your water provider's emergency number and ask them to shut off the supply. Then call a plumber to replace the valve. In the meantime, you can shut off individual fixtures at their stops and turn off the water heater to limit damage.

Shut off water to the water heater and appliances

A failing water heater can flood a garage or utility closet. Know where its cold water inlet valve is and close it if the tank leaks. Turn off the power or gas to the unit before you drain it, and read the label for the correct steps. The page on water heater leak cleanup explains what comes next.

Washing machines, dishwashers and ice makers have their own small shutoff valves. Close the washer valves when you leave on a long trip. A braided hose can fail at any age, and a closed valve means a failure cannot flood the room.

Practice and label

Test your main shutoff once a year. Turn it off, open a faucet to confirm the flow stops, then turn it back on slowly. If it is stiff, have a plumber service or replace it before an emergency.

Tag the valve with a bright label and tell everyone in the house. If you rent in Mesquite or Forney, ask your landlord where it is and keep the information with your lease. A burst pipe on a weekend is not the time to start searching.

Know your electrical panel too

Water and electricity do not mix. If water is flowing near outlets, appliances or the panel, do not stand in it to reach a switch. If you can reach the breaker box safely and dry, turn off the circuits for the affected area.

If the panel itself is wet or the water is deep, stay out and call your electric provider or an electrician. Safety comes before saving belongings. Once the power is off, you can focus on stopping the water and calling for help.

What to do after the water is off

Shutting off the main only stops new water. The water already in the house keeps damaging floors and walls, so the next step is removal and drying. Open drawers and cabinet doors, lift furniture and move belongings to higher ground.

Call for burst pipe water damage help and your insurance company. Take photos, keep the broken pipe section if you can and write down when you shut off the water. That timeline helps both the repair and the claim.

Smart devices and low-cost protection

Water sensors that sound an alarm or send a phone alert cost little and sit under sinks, near water heaters and beside washing machines. Some systems include an automatic shutoff valve on the main line. They can limit damage when no one is home.

These devices complement, but do not replace, knowing where your manual valve is. Batteries die and Wi-Fi drops. A simple habit of checking the manual valve once a year is still the most reliable plan.

Local note: ask your provider

The City of Forney and other Kaufman County water providers publish customer service contacts and policies on their official sites. Those pages say who handles meter-side valves and emergency shutoffs for your address.

Texas utilities commonly separate the utility side of the meter from the customer side, so confirm with your own provider before you operate any valve in the meter box.
