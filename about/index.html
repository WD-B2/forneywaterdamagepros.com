About Forney Water Damage Pros

A local water damage service on US 80 in Forney, Texas, with an emergency line that is answered 24/7.

Call (844) 950-6900

Who we are

Forney Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service based at 724 E US Highway 80 in Forney, Texas. We answer an emergency line 24/7 for homes and businesses in Forney, Terrell, Mesquite, Seagoville and the towns along US 80, and we line up licensed and insured restoration crews as availability allows.

We started this site because water damage is a local problem with local causes. Clay soil, heavy spring rain, rare but hard freezes and storms that cross Kaufman and Dallas counties all show up in the calls we take. The pages here are written around those facts, not around a template.

How we work

Every job starts with a phone call about what happened. We ask where the water came from, how long it has been there and what it touched, then we explain what to shut off and what to leave alone. A crew inspects with moisture meters and a thermal camera and explains the scope and price before work starts.

The work follows the published industry standards. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers water damage restoration and S520 covers mold remediation. Standards are the reason a crew classifies water, dries to measured readings and does not close a wall until it is dry. You can ask any company, including us, whether the crew works to them.

What we do and do not do

An honest list builds trust more than a long one.

  • We extract water, dry structures and handle cleanup and repair of water-damaged materials.
  • We do not replace roofs, repair plumbing or service air conditioners. Those trades fix the source, and we work around them.
  • We do not give legal or insurance advice. Ask your insurer what your policy covers.
  • We do not promise arrival times, because they depend on the night you call.
  • We do not claim a certain outcome before a crew has looked at the damage.

Where we are in Forney

Forney is about 20 miles from downtown Dallas on US 80, founded in 1871 and incorporated in 1884. The city calls Historic Downtown the heart of the community, and the Spellman Museum of Forney History, the Forney Arts House and Hamblen Park behind City Hall are all there. When callers use those places to tell us where they are, we can place the call in a sentence.

If you want to talk through a problem before you decide anything, call the line. See the full list of services, the towns we cover or the contact page.

What happens when you call

The same sequence applies on a Tuesday afternoon and a Sunday at 3 a.m.

  1. You describe the water

    Where it started, what it touched and whether anything electrical is wet.

  2. We give safety steps

    What to shut off, what to leave alone and what to photograph.

  3. A crew is lined up

    Based on availability, with the scope and price explained before work starts.

  4. The crew measures

    Moisture meters and a thermal camera show what is wet before anything is removed.

  5. Drying is confirmed

    Readings are logged until the structure reaches its dry target.

Why we publish guides

The blog on this site exists because most people call us after a leak, a storm or a stain, and they have questions that do not need a crew. How long does drying take. Does insurance pay.

Can a wet sofa be saved. We answer those questions in plain language and point to the primary sources behind them.

Those sources include the Texas Department of Insurance, the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency, the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation and the ANSI/IICRC standards. When a guide cites a rule, you can look up the rule yourself. We would rather you check than take our word for it.

Questions to ask any restoration company

These work whether you call us or someone else.

  • Will the crew measure moisture before and after, and will I see the readings?
  • What does the written scope include, and what is left out?
  • How will you tell me about price changes if the job grows?
  • Who is on site, and are subcontractors involved?
  • What records will I get for my insurance claim?

A company that answers these clearly is easier to trust than one that wants a signature first.

Homes, rentals and businesses

The line takes calls from homeowners, renters, landlords, property managers and small businesses. A rental owner needs documentation for an insurer and a tenant. A shop owner needs the space back in use. A homeowner needs the family safe and the house dry.

Each call is different, so we ask about your situation instead of reading a script. Commercial calls are covered on the commercial water damage restoration page, and the rest follow the service pages linked from the menu.
