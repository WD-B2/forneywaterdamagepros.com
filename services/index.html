Find the service that matches your water damage

Water damage rarely arrives with a label, so this page sorts our services by cause. Start with where the water came from. A split pipe, a failed appliance, a leaking roof and rising flood water all need different first steps, even though the cleanup that follows looks similar.

If you are not sure what to pick, call the 24/7 line and describe the stain, the smell or the water. We will tell you which page applies and what to do while help is lined up. The towns we cover are on the service area page.

Pipes and plumbing

Water that comes from inside the supply lines or the tank.

Burst Pipe Water Damage

Shut the water off, extract what spilled and dry the walls and floors that took it.

Frozen Pipe Water Damage

Water damage after a hard freeze splits a line inside a wall, attic or slab.

Water Heater Leak Cleanup

Tank failures and slow leaks in the closet, garage or attic, cleaned up and dried.

Slab Leak Water Damage

Water coming up through the floor or a hot spot on the slab, traced and dried out.

Appliances, HVAC and kitchens

Leaks from equipment and fixtures that run all day without anyone noticing.

Appliance Leak Water Damage

Washer, dishwasher and refrigerator leaks that soak floors and cabinet bases.

AC Leak Water Damage

Clogged drain lines and overflowing pans that stain ceilings and soak closets.

Kitchen Water Damage

Under-sink leaks, cabinet bases and kitchen floors that stayed wet too long.

Weather and building envelope

Water that arrives from outside, through the roof, the walls or the ground.

Flood Damage Cleanup

Rain and runoff that enters the home, handled as contaminated water from the first step.

Ceiling Water Damage Repair

Stains, sags and drips from roof leaks, upstairs plumbing and attic condensation.

Crawl Space Water Damage

Wet insulation, damp joists and pooled water under raised floors.

Storm Water Damage

Wind-driven rain, roof openings and storm runoff after severe North Texas weather.

Basement Water Damage

Seepage and flooding in below-grade rooms, pumped out and dried down to the slab.

Water removal and drying

The core work of getting water out and getting the structure dry.

Water Extraction

Standing water pulled out fast, then the structure dried so damage stops spreading.

Sewage Backup Cleanup

Contaminated water removed and affected materials cleaned, sanitized or discarded.

Structural Drying

Air movers, dehumidifiers and moisture readings until the structure is actually dry.
