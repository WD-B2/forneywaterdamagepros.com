Ceiling water emergency: a bulge means water is pooled above

A ceiling that is bulging, dripping or turning brown is an emergency because water is collecting above it. Our 24/7 line is open. While you wait, move people and furniture out from under the sag and switch off any light fixtures in the wet area. Ceiling water damage repair means finding where the water is coming from, drying the cavity and insulation above, and then repairing the drywall, texture and paint below. The source decides everything. A roof leak, an upstairs plumbing leak and a clogged AC condensate line each leave a similar stain.

Texas heat plays a part. NOAA's 1991 to 2020 normals show about 20 days a year at 100 degrees or more at DFW, so air conditioners run hard, and attic units can overflow a pan or clog a drain line. Many Forney ceiling stains trace back to condensate water, not rain.

How to read a ceiling stain

The shape and timing give clues about the source.

  • A stain that appears after rain points to the roof or flashing.
  • A stain that grows on dry days often points to plumbing or an AC condensate line.
  • A stain below a bathroom or laundry room points to supply or drain lines upstairs.
  • Rings with darker edges mean the area got wet more than once.
  • Musty smell with no stain can mean wet insulation above.

If you suspect the AC, see the AC leak page. If the water came through a roof opening, the storm water damage page covers that side.

Safety steps while you wait for help

  • Move people, pets and furniture out of the room under the stain.
  • Do not turn on ceiling lights or fans that are wet or near the wet area.
  • Turn off power to the affected room at the panel if you can reach it safely.
  • If water is dripping from a light fixture, stay out and call for help.

How a ceiling is dried and repaired

Done in this order, the repair lasts.

  1. Stop the source

    A roofer, plumber or HVAC tech repairs the cause. No repair holds if water is still coming.

  2. Release trapped water

    Small relief holes or cutouts let pooled water drain and show how far it spread.

  3. Dry the cavity

    Insulation is removed if wet, and air movers dry joists and drywall from below and above.

  4. Verify dry

    Moisture meters confirm the framing is dry before closing the ceiling.

  5. Repair and refinish

    New drywall, texture matching and paint finish the job. Staining shows through if it is painted over wet.

Mistakes that make ceiling damage worse

Painting over a stain is the most common mistake. The paint hides the problem for a few weeks, then the stain bleeds through because the drywall or insulation above never dried. Closing the ceiling too early is the second. If wet insulation stays sealed in, it keeps the cavity damp and invites mold.

The third is waiting to see whether the stain grows. A stain that is still spreading means water is still arriving, and every day adds more wet material. A short call is a cheap way to find out, and the answer might simply be that a roofer or HVAC technician should come first.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard asks a crew to classify the water and dry the cavity above the stain, not only the visible spot. Callers in Mesquite and the rest of the area reach the same line.
