Flood emergency: why this is not ordinary water

Flood water is an emergency from the moment it enters a home. It comes from outside, carries soil, bacteria and chemicals, and is treated as contaminated no matter how clear it looks. Our 24/7 line is open, and while you wait you should stay out of the water, avoid touching wet electrical equipment and keep children and pets away.

Flood damage cleanup is the removal of outside water from a structure, followed by cleaning, sanitizing and drying. Porous materials that soaked in flood water, such as carpet, pad and insulation, are usually removed because they cannot be reliably cleaned. Hard surfaces and framing can often be saved.

Kaufman County sits on a divide between the Trinity and Sabine river basins, and the Texas State Historical Association notes that the East Fork of the Trinity River drains the county's western half, where Forney sits. Lake Ray Hubbard lies about four miles northwest. Heavy rain on clay soil that drains slowly means runoff can pool at low spots, driveways and garage thresholds.

Safety first when flood water enters

These steps matter more than speed.

  • Do not walk through the water until the power is off. Cut breakers only if the panel is dry. If not, call the utility.
  • Do not use household appliances or run fans in standing water.
  • Wear boots and gloves if you must enter, and wash hands and skin after contact.
  • Do not eat food that touched flood water, and throw out anything unsealed.
  • Document the water line on the walls with photos before cleanup.

Call 911 for anything involving gas or live electrical hazards before you call us.

Flood insurance: what homeowners often miss

The Texas Department of Insurance says standard homeowner and renter policies usually do not cover flood damage, and they usually exclude mold that results from a flood. Flood coverage is a separate policy, commonly through the National Flood Insurance Program or a private insurer. If you do not have one, call your agent and the TDI help line at 1-800-252-3439 to understand what applies.

The FEMA Flood Map Service Center shows the flood zone for an address, and your city building department can explain local rules for repairs. We do not guess a flood zone for you, because the answer is address specific. Check the map and keep your policy handy when you call your insurer.

What changes the cost of flood cleanup

Flood cleanup is priced by what the flood did, not by a flat rate.

  • Water categoryFlood water is Category 3, so protective gear, disposal and sanitizing are always part of the job.
  • Depth and areaA few inches in one room is different from a foot through a ground floor.
  • Materials removedCarpet, pad and lower drywall add tear-out and disposal.
  • DryingStructural drying runs days and depends on the volume of wet material.
  • RebuildReplacing drywall, baseboards and flooring is priced separately.
