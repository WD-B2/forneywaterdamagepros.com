Burst pipe emergency: what to do in the first ten minutes

A burst pipe is an emergency because the water keeps coming until someone closes a valve. Our 24/7 line is open, but the first action is yours: shut off the main water valve, then open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain the line. If water is near the electrical panel or outlets, keep out and cut power only if you can reach the panel dry.

Burst pipe water damage is the cleanup and drying that follows a split or failed supply line. It covers water extraction, removing materials that cannot be saved, drying the wall cavity and floor system, and checking for moisture that traveled beyond the wet room.

Finding the main valve is where most people lose time. In Forney the City of Forney runs the water system, with treated water bought from the North Texas Municipal Water District. Your home should have its own main shutoff, usually near the front foundation, in the garage or by the water heater.

If you cannot find or turn it, the meter box near the street has a shutoff, and the city utility office can tell you who may operate it.

Before the crew arrives

These five actions limit the damage without putting you at risk.

Stop the water

Close the main valve. If the valve is stuck, call a plumber or the city for the meter shutoff.

Protect the power

Turn off breakers for wet rooms only if the panel is dry and easy to reach. Otherwise leave it and call 911 or the utility.

Lift what you can

Move electronics, papers, shoes and small furniture off wet floors. Leave heavy furniture where it is.

Capture the evidence

Take photos and video of the damage and the pipe before anything is moved. Your insurer will ask.

Open the house

Open cabinet doors under sinks and run fans if the power is safe. Air movement helps while you wait.

Call the line

Tell us which rooms are wet and whether water came through a ceiling. That decides what equipment goes on the truck.

Why the Feb 2021 freeze still matters here

The February 2021 freeze is the reason many Forney homeowners now think about pipes before winter. The National Weather Service in Fort Worth recorded a low of minus 2 degrees at Dallas-Fort Worth on February 16, 2021, and NOAA's climate records note that the freeze burst water pipes across Texas and led to boil water advisories. Pipes in attics and exterior walls took the worst of it.

Most burst pipes are not freeze events, though. Aging supply lines, worn braided connectors and high water pressure cause failures in every season. The practical point is the same either way: a pipe inside a wall can leak for hours before a stain shows, so check the ceiling below and the baseboards beside any wet wall.

The extraction and drying steps below follow the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for professional water damage restoration. If the failed part was a tank and not a pipe, the water heater leak page covers that case.

What gets checked after the water stops

The visible puddle is the smallest part of a burst pipe loss.

  • The wall cavity around the break, opened with a small cut if the drywall is wet.
  • The floor system and any insulation below, because water follows gravity.
  • Ceilings and rooms below the break, which often hold more water than the room above.
  • Cabinet bases, baseboards and trim, which wick water upward.
  • The pipe itself, so a plumber can repair the cause before drying starts.

Drying is covered on the structural drying page, and a split inside a wall often leads to the repairs described on the wall and drywall page.

What drives the cost of burst pipe cleanup

No two burst pipes cost the same. The loss itself sets the number.

  • How long it ranA pipe closed in minutes soaks one room. One that ran all night soaks the house.
  • Where it burstInside a wall or ceiling means opening finished surfaces. Under a sink is simpler.
  • Materials soakedHardwood, carpet pad and insulation cost more to dry or replace than tile.
  • Drying timeLarger wet areas need more equipment for more days.
  • Repairs after dryingPaint, drywall and flooring are priced separately from mitigation.

Insurers commonly treat a sudden pipe burst as a covered loss, while the cost of the pipe repair itself is often excluded. Ask your insurer which parts apply. Want your real number?
