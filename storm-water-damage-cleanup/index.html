Storm emergency: when to call and when to wait

A storm that opens the roof or breaks a window lets rain in until the opening is covered, so water damage can start while the storm is still going. Our 24/7 line is open. While the weather is dangerous, stay in a safe room, keep away from windows and do not go outside to check the roof.

Once it is safe, move belongings away from the water, catch drips with buckets and photograph the damage.

Storm water damage cleanup is the removal and drying of water that entered through wind, hail or flood damage, along with temporary protection of openings. It sits between roof repair and water cleanup. A roofer closes the opening and we handle the water inside.

What Forney has seen from severe weather

On April 3, 2012, an EF-3 tornado struck near Forney. A roof opened by wind lets rain into attics and ceilings, and one lesson from that event is how quickly secondary water damage follows structural damage. After any severe storm, check the attic and top-floor ceilings as soon as it is safe, because water can pool for hours before a stain shows.

Before the crew arrives

These steps limit damage without putting you at risk.

Stay safe

Limit the water

After the storm

Storm water often shows up as a ceiling stain or wet insulation. When runoff enters at ground level, it follows the rules on the flood damage page. Drying is the heart of the job, as explained on the structural drying page.

If wet materials stay wet, mold remediation may be needed. We also serve Seagoville storm calls.

Checks to make once the storm has passed

Walk the house after the weather clears and look for stains on ceilings and the upper parts of walls, which are usually the first sign that the roof leaked. Check windows and doors for water along sills, and look in the attic for damp insulation or daylight where there should be none.

From the ground, look for missing shingles, lifted flashing and debris on the roof, but do not climb up while it is wet. Photograph what you see with the date visible, because your insurer will ask for evidence, and keep receipts for emergency repairs such as tarps and board-up.
